Overview

The Clean That Goes Deeper

Deep cleaning goes well beyond the surface. We target hard-to-reach areas, baseboards, light fixtures, vents, grout and the commonly overlooked spots where dust and grime collect over time. Hard-water stains, soap scum and kitchen buildup are tackled head-on.

It is the ideal first visit before starting a recurring plan, a seasonal refresh, or a reset after a busy stretch. When we are done, your whole space feels brand new again.

FAQ

Questions About Deep Cleaning

How is a deep clean different from a standard clean?add

A deep clean includes everything in a standard clean plus detailed work on baseboards, fixtures, grout, vents, appliance detailing and buildup removal. It takes longer and is much more thorough.

How often should I schedule a deep clean?add

Most homes benefit from a deep clean once or twice a year, with standard or recurring cleanings in between.

Can I add inside the oven or refrigerator?add

Yes. Interior oven and refrigerator cleaning are available as add-ons to any deep clean.
